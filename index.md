# Privacy Policy for When is pay?

**Effective date: October 2, 2026**

When is pay? is a personal payday and budgeting application designed to help users track their payday countdown, available balance, expenses, bills, pay cycles, and safe-to-spend amounts.

## Information you enter

When is pay? allows you to enter information such as your payday and pay schedule, expected and received pay, current balance, expenses, bills and recurring bills, payday buffer, and other information used to calculate your available money and spending amounts.

This information is stored locally on your device.

**When is pay? does not transmit this financial information to the developer or to an external server.**

## Accounts

When is pay? does not require you to create an account or sign in.

## Internet access

The current version of When is pay? does not request Android's Internet permission and does not use an internet connection to transmit your financial information.

## Notifications

When is pay? may request permission to send notifications.

If enabled, notifications may provide payday reminders and information such as the number of days until payday and budgeting information based on data stored in the app.

You can control notification permissions through your device settings and control available reminder options within the app.

Financial amounts can be hidden from notifications using the app's privacy settings.

If you create a personal notification message, that message is stored locally on your device and may appear in notifications according to your selected settings.

## Device restart and reminders

The app may receive a notification from the Android operating system after your device restarts. This is used to restore scheduled reminders.

This functionality is not used to collect or transmit personal information.

## Data sharing

The developer of When is pay? does not sell, share, or transmit the financial information you enter into the app to third parties.

## Data retention and deletion

Your app data is stored locally on your device.

You can remove information using the available controls within the app. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored app data according to Android's normal app-data handling.

## Children

When is pay? is a general personal finance utility and is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

## Security

Because your budgeting information is stored locally, the security of that information also depends on the security of your device. You should use appropriate device security measures, such as a screen lock, where necessary.

## Changes to this privacy policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the features or data practices of When is pay? change. Any updated version will be made available at the privacy policy URL associated with the app's Google Play listing.
